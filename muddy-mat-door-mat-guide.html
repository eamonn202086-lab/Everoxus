What Makes a Doormat Actually Work for Muddy Paws

  • Microfiber or chenille pile — traps dirt and moisture down in the fibres instead of leaving it sitting on the surface
  • Non-slip backing — a mat that slides on hard flooring gets kicked out of position within days
  • Quick-dry material — a mat that stays damp all day just spreads mud around instead of absorbing it
  • Machine washable — non-negotiable for anything catching mud daily

Where to put it

One mat directly outside the door your dog uses most does more work than several mats spread around the house. If your dog has a regular route in from the yard, that's the highest-value spot — every wipe there is mud that never makes it to the hallway.

Care tips

  • Shake out loose dirt before washing to avoid clogging the machine filter
  • Cold wash, air dry where possible — high heat can flatten the pile over time
  • Keep a second mat in rotation if it's catching mud daily, so one is always dry and ready

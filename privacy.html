Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Everoxus is a small, independent website based in Australia that recommends dog products sold on Amazon. There are no accounts, sign-up forms or newsletters here, and we never ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.

Some services we use do collect information automatically when you visit. This page explains what they are and how to control them.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit, which pages they read and roughly where they are visiting from. It uses cookies and collects details such as the pages you view, your device and browser type and an approximate location. Your IP address is processed by Google. We only see combined statistics, not who you are. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Amazon and other affiliate links

Everoxus participates in the Amazon Associates Program. When you click a link to Amazon, Amazon may use cookies to record that you came from this site, so we can earn a commission if you buy something. We receive reports of total orders and earnings, not your name or what you personally bought. Some pages also use Impact, an affiliate tracking service, which may set cookies to record referrals to brands. Once you are on Amazon or another retailer's site, their own privacy policy applies.

Pinterest

The Save to Pinterest buttons are plain links. Nothing is sent to Pinterest unless you tap one, and after that Pinterest's own privacy policy applies.

Fonts and hosting

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page loads. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

Cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. The site still works without them.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal information, run advertising networks on the site, or collect anything that would let us identify you.

Orders and purchases

Everoxus doesn't sell anything directly. Orders, payments, deliveries, returns and product questions are handled by Amazon and the seller you bought from, so please contact them.

Australian privacy law

We aim to handle any personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. You can learn more about your privacy rights from the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Benebone Wishbone vs Maplestick — Which One Does Your Dog Actually Need?

By Everoxus · Updated August 2026 · #ad affiliate links

Benebone's chew toys have built a reputation for surviving dogs that destroy everything else. Two of their most popular shapes — the Wishbone and the Maplestick — get compared constantly, and the honest answer is they're not really competing with each other. They're built for different jobs.

The Real Difference Isn't Softness — It's Shape and Size

A common assumption is that one of these is a "softer puppy version" of the other. That's not accurate — both are made from the same durable nylon material. The real differences are shape (wishbone's curved ends vs maplestick's straight profile) and the size range each comes in, from tiny up to giant. For a puppy, the right move is picking a smaller size in whichever shape your dog prefers, not assuming one product is inherently gentler than the other.

Which one should you actually buy?

  • Dog likes to hold toys between their paws — Wishbone's curved ends give more to grip
  • Dog prefers a straight stick shape — Maplestick is the closer match to an actual stick
  • Puppy or small dog — pick the smallest appropriate size in whichever shape they respond to, not a separate "puppy" product
  • Not sure — the Wishbone has the larger review base and broader flavour appeal (bacon vs wood), so it's the safer default if you're choosing blind

Our Verdict

Both are durable — the shape is the real decision

Neither Benebone shape is objectively better than the other; they're built from the same tough nylon and hold up the same way under real chewing. Let your dog's chewing style pick the shape, and size down rather than assuming a separate softer product exists for puppies.

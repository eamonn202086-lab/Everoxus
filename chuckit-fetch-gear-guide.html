Best Chuckit Fetch Gear 2026 — Ball, Stick or Launcher?

By Everoxus · Updated August 2026 · #ad affiliate links

Chuckit built its name on one idea: fetch is better when you don't have to bend down or handle a slobbery ball. The brand now covers three different formats — a classic ball, a fetch stick, and a launcher — and between them they've earned over 180,000 combined Amazon reviews. The right pick depends less on which is "best" and more on your dog's size and how far you actually want to throw.

Which Format Fits Your Dog

  • Ball — the default choice for most dogs. Easy to fetch, easy to hold, works with or without a launcher
  • Fetch stick — better grip for dogs that prefer sticks over balls, and easier for you to spot in long grass
  • Launcher — adds real distance and keeps ball-slobber off your hands entirely. Best if your dog has more energy than your throwing arm can handle

Do you need the launcher too, or just a ball?

If your dog is content with a normal throwing distance, the Ultra Ball on its own covers most fetch sessions. The launcher earns its place for high-energy dogs that need more ground covered per throw than a human arm can manage over 20+ repeat throws, or for anyone who'd rather not pick a slobbery ball off the ground by hand.

Our Verdict

Start with the ball, add the others as needed

The Ultra Ball is the right starting point for almost every dog — durable, cheap to replace, and by far the most reviewed of the three. Add the fetch stick if your dog prefers sticks to balls, and the launcher if fetch sessions are wearing out your arm before they wear out your dog.

Why Dogs Need to Chew

Chewing is a natural and necessary behaviour for dogs. It helps keep teeth clean, relieves anxiety, combats boredom, and provides mental stimulation that physical exercise alone doesn't deliver. A dog that doesn't have appropriate things to chew will find inappropriate ones — furniture, shoes, cables.

The right chew toy redirects that instinct productively. The wrong one either gets destroyed in minutes or doesn't engage the dog at all.

What Makes a Good Chew Toy

  • Material — natural rubber is the gold standard. It's durable, non-toxic, and has the right resistance for satisfying chewing without damaging teeth
  • Size — too small is a choking hazard, too large and the dog loses interest. Match the toy size to your dog's breed and weight
  • Treat compatibility — toys that can be filled with treats extend engagement significantly. A dog will work on a treat-filled toy for far longer than a plain one
  • Durability — the toy needs to survive sustained chewing without breaking into pieces that could be swallowed

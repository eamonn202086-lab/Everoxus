You've done the responsible thing and picked up after your dog — now you're stuck holding a tied-off bag for the rest of the walk, or worse, swinging it as you go. A small accessory box that clips to your leash solves this specific, small, daily annoyance completely. Attach it once and forget about it.

Skip it if…

What It Actually Solves

This isn't a general-purpose gadget — it does one job well. It attaches directly to a Flexi retractable lead (or any standard leash with the right fitting) and gives you a dedicated compartment to store a fresh bag roll, and somewhere to stash a used one until you reach a bin. No more pockets, no more carrying it in your free hand, no more forgetting a bag at home.

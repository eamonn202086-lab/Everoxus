What to Look for in a Dog Poop Bag

Most dog owners don't think about poop bags until they have a problem. Here's what actually matters:

  • Thickness — thin bags split. You want at least 15 microns of thickness for reliable leak protection
  • Size — bags need to be long enough to tie off cleanly. Undersized bags are frustrating and messy
  • Easy to open — fumbling with a bag in the dark or cold is miserable. Perforations matter
  • Scent — a light scent makes the experience significantly more pleasant. Lavender is the gold standard
  • Environmental impact — recycled or biodegradable materials are worth considering if this matters to you

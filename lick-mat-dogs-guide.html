If your dog gets anxious during thunderstorms, vet visits, grooming sessions, or when guests arrive, a lick mat is one of the most effective and underrated tools available. Why Licking Calms Dogs Down

Licking is a self-soothing behaviour in dogs. It triggers the release of endorphins — the same feel-good chemicals released during exercise — which reduces stress and anxiety naturally. Vets and animal behaviourists recommend lick mats as a drug-free way to manage mild to moderate anxiety in dogs.

Beyond anxiety relief, lick mats also slow down fast eaters, provide mental enrichment, and keep dogs occupied during situations where you need them calm and distracted — nail trims, baths, vet checks, and busy periods at home.

What to Look for in a Lick Mat

  • Texture variety — different texture zones keep dogs engaged longer and make the food harder to lick out quickly
  • Suction cups — a mat that sticks to the floor or wall stays in place during enthusiastic licking and is particularly useful for bath time
  • Food-grade silicone — safe, flexible, and easy to clean
  • Dishwasher safe — essential for daily use
  • Freezer safe — freezing the filled mat extends engagement significantly

Best foods to use on a lick mat

  • Peanut butter — make sure it contains no xylitol, which is toxic to dogs. Most standard peanut butters are fine
  • Plain Greek yogurt — high protein, dogs love it, freezes well
  • Pumpkin puree — plain canned pumpkin is great for digestion and works perfectly on a lick mat
  • Wet dog food — spread a thin layer and freeze for a longer-lasting mat
  • Mashed banana — simple, natural, and most dogs find it irresistible

The freezing technique

Spreading food on the mat and handing it straight to your dog will keep them busy for 5-10 minutes. Freezing the filled mat overnight extends that to 20-30 minutes or longer. For maximum effect during high-anxiety situations — thunderstorms, fireworks, vet visits — prepare a frozen mat the night before and have it ready.

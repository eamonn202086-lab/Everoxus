Tips That Actually Speed Up Potty Training

  • Consistency beats everything — take your puppy outside at the same times every day. After meals, after naps, after play. Routine is what builds the habit.
  • Reward immediately — when your puppy goes in the right place, reward within 2 seconds. Delayed rewards don't connect the behaviour to the outcome.
  • Never punish accidents — puppies don't connect punishment to something they did minutes ago. It creates anxiety without teaching anything useful.
  • Reduce the space — the more space a puppy has to roam unsupervised, the more accidents happen. Use the playpen to keep the area manageable.
  • Watch for signals — sniffing the ground, circling, and squatting are the three warning signs. Learn to read them and you'll catch most accidents before they happen.
